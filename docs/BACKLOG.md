# Backlog

Future ideas the owner explicitly approved keeping. An entry is not approved version scope, a promise, implementation permission, or evidence of feasibility. Discussion and agent suggestions are not approval.

When an idea becomes approved release scope, move its done-means into the ledger and its approved rules into decisions. Preserve the approval reference.

## Entry format

```markdown
### IDEA-001: <short name>
- **Status:** Deferred | Next up | Needs research
- **Approved:** <date and owner message/reference>
- **Direction:** <problem and the direction the owner chose>
- **Open questions:** <what must be resolved before scoping>
```
